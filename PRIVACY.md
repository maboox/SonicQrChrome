# SonicQR privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026.

The complete Persian and English policy is in `privacy.html`. It can be read inside the extension and published with `privacy.css` on the publisher's HTTPS site.

SonicQR processes message text, an optional encryption/decryption password, microphone input requested by the destination site or internal test, selected audio files, and user-initiated target-tab audio locally. The password itself is not included in the encoded voice. The selected tab's URL, title and ID are used only to identify and operate on that target. Recording counters, timing and output levels support local diagnostics.

The extension has no accounts, backend, analytics, advertising or automatic network uploads. It does not read browsing-history APIs, cookies, contacts or clipboard contents, and it does not scrape site conversations or unrelated page text. Only language preference is saved in local extension storage; target IDs are in session storage. Chrome retains optional site grants. Temporary text, password and audio remain in the relevant page/operation memory. Closing or reloading that page releases its memory; users control deletion of downloads and copied clipboard content. Uninstalling removes extension settings.

When you arm mixing, the destination site receives mixed audio. Sending that recording through the site uses that site's service and privacy policy. Unencrypted text can be decoded by anyone with a preserved recording. Use an optional password and share it separately for private text; encryption does not hide signal presence.

Recovered text and sanitized diagnostics enter the clipboard only after a Copy action. Reports contain the version, destination origin, states, counters, duration and output level, but no message, password, packet or audio samples. No reports are sent automatically. Support information that you voluntarily send is used to answer your request.

Data is used only for text-in-voice functionality and requested troubleshooting. It is not sold, used for ads, profiling or credit decisions. Optional site grants can be removed in the panel; reload the site to remove already loaded hooks. Revoke microphone access in browser/OS settings. Material data-handling changes must be reflected in a new extension and policy version. For questions, use the repository's support page at https://github.com/maboox/SonicQrChrome/issues.

## خلاصهٔ فارسی

متن، رمز و صوت در بخش مربوط به عملیات روی دستگاه پردازش می‌شوند. افزونه سرور، حساب کاربری، تبلیغ یا ارسال خودکار گزارش ندارد. نشانی و عنوان تب فقط برای مقصد انتخابی استفاده می‌شوند؛ تاریخچهٔ مرورگر، کوکی‌ها، گفتگوها و کلیپ‌بورد خوانده نمی‌شوند. فقط زبان و شناسهٔ مقصد ذخیره می‌شوند و Chrome اجازهٔ اختیاری هر سایت را نگه می‌دارد. دانلود و کپی فقط با اقدام شما انجام می‌شوند.

سایت مقصد به صوت ترکیبی دسترسی دارد و ارسال وویس از طریق سرویس خود سایت انجام می‌شود. متن بدون رمز از فایل صوتی سالم قابل استخراج است. برای محرمانگی رمز تعیین و آن را جدا منتقل کنید. گزارش محلی عیب‌یابی فاقد متن، رمز، بسته و صوت است. نسخهٔ کامل سیاست، حقوق انتخاب و حذف و مسیر تماس ناشر در `privacy.html` آمده است.
